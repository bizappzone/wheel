With unlimited budget enabling aggressive acquisition via Facebook groups (4M+ members), influencers, and conferences, launch prioritizes US STEM for virality, expanding to elementary/special ed. Why now: Post-pandemic shifts and shortages make reciprocity timely, positioning us for 25% subscriber advocacy and 0.4-0.6 viral coefficient—execute to own the creator-powered subscription quadrant.

## 7. Module Specifications

### 1. Content Management Module
**Purpose:** Technical specification for the Content Management Module

**Key Features:**
1. Multi-file upload with drag-and-drop interface supporting PDF, DOCX, PPT, images, and video formats with file size validation and virus scanning
2. Strict hierarchical taxonomy system with grade levels (K-12), subjects (Math, Science, ELA, Social Studies, STEM, Arts), and sub-topics mapped to curriculum standards
3. Automated DRM application and visible/invisible watermarking with uploader attribution and download tracking for IP protection
4. Advanced search and filtering by grade, subject, resource type, High Demand tags, popularity metrics, and upload date with saved search functionality
5. Resource preview capability with watermarked thumbnails and limited page views before credit redemption
6. Bulk upload and editing tools for power users managing large resource libraries
7. Version control for resource updates with change tracking and notification to previous downloaders
8. Resource collections and bundling to group related materials together
9. Duplicate detection using content hashing to prevent redundant uploads
10. Download history tracking per resource with anonymized usage analytics for creators
